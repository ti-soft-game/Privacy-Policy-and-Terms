Privacy Policy for TIGames Mobile Apps

Effective date: 29 September 2026

This policy applies to the Android apps Resume Card, Home Part Pocket, and BackPin, published by TIGames. These apps do not require an account, do not operate a developer server, and do not include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, cloud-sync, or purchase SDKs in version 1.0.

Information stored on your device

Resume Card stores the cards, history, timers, settings, and optional exported backup data that you create. Home Part Pocket stores household part names, categories, specifications, locations, notes, replacement information, and settings that you enter. This information stays in the app’s private storage on your device.

You may delete local app information by using the available in-app deletion controls, clearing the app’s storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the app.

BackPin location and photos

BackPin asks for foreground approximate or precise location only after you tap its location button. It uses one current location to save or guide you back to a return point. BackPin does not request background location, does not continuously track you, and does not send location to TIGames.

When you choose to add a photo, BackPin opens the system camera. The photo you choose is stored locally with your return point. BackPin does not request broad gallery or storage access.

Sharing you initiate

Resume Card can create a backup file when you choose Export. BackPin can open a map app with a selected coordinate or open the Android share sheet for a card. The receiving app or service is chosen by you and is governed by its own privacy policy. TIGames does not receive the exported or shared information.

Advertising and children

Version 1.0 of these apps does not show advertising and does not include an advertising SDK. The apps are not designed for children. If a future version adds advertising, network services, analytics, or new data use, this policy and the relevant Google Play disclosures will be updated before release.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy before a changed practice takes effect. For questions, contact ti.game.2020@gmail.com.
