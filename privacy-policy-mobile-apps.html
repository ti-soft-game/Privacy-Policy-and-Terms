Privacy Policy for TIGames Mobile Apps and Games

Effective date: 3 October 2026

This policy applies to Android apps and games published by TIGames, including Neon Bounce: Cyber Ricochet, Resume Card, Home Part Pocket, and BackPin. No TIGames app requires an account.

Neon Bounce advertising

Neon Bounce version 1.0.1 and later may show advertising to eligible adult users through Appodeal and advertising networks enabled in Appodeal mediation. Rewarded video is optional and grants the displayed in-game reward only after the video is completed. Occasional interstitial ads may be shown between completed game sessions, not during active gameplay.

For advertising, the Appodeal SDK and enabled mediation partners may collect or process advertising ID, IP address, device and network information, ad impressions and clicks, app interactions, diagnostics, and other app performance information. These signals are used to deliver and measure advertising, prevent fraud, operate the SDK, and provide advertising analytics. TIGames does not request location permission for advertising and does not provide a user ID, name, email address, purchase history, contacts, photos, or files to Appodeal.

Appodeal's privacy practices are described in the Appodeal Privacy Policy. Consent choices may be requested by Appodeal's consent manager where required. Eligible users can reopen available privacy choices from the in-game menu.

Age eligibility

Before the advertising SDK can start, Neon Bounce asks for a date of birth or offers an option to play without advertising. The full date is evaluated only on the device and is not stored or transmitted. The app stores only whether advertising is allowed. Appodeal is not initialized for minors, users of unknown age, or users who choose the no-ad option.

Information stored on your device

Neon Bounce stores game progress, settings, earned virtual currency, unlocked cosmetic items, daily missions, rewards, and the advertising eligibility choice on the device. Resume Card stores cards, history, timers, settings, and optional exported backup data. Home Part Pocket stores household part details and settings. This information stays in the app's private storage unless a feature explicitly described above sends limited advertising information.

You may delete local app information by using available in-app controls, clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the app.

BackPin location and photos

BackPin asks for foreground approximate or precise location only after you tap its location button. It uses one current location to save or guide you back to a return point. BackPin does not request background location, continuously track you, or send location to TIGames.

When you choose to add a photo, BackPin opens the system camera. The selected photo is stored locally with the return point. BackPin does not request broad gallery or storage access.

Sharing you initiate

Resume Card can create a backup file when you choose Export. BackPin can open a map app with a selected coordinate or the Android share sheet for a card. The receiving app or service is chosen by you and governed by its own privacy policy. TIGames does not receive the exported or shared information.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy before a changed practice takes effect. For questions, contact ti.game.2020@gmail.com.
