Privacy Policy for Ten-Minute Home

Effective date: 15 September 2026

Ten-Minute Home helps you create and complete short home-reset plans. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your selected rooms, planning preferences, task progress, session history, reminder choices, and theme settings locally on your device so its features work. Ten-Minute Home does not require an account and does not operate a server for this information.

Backups you choose to export

When you choose Export backup, the app creates a backup file containing your app data and opens your device's share sheet. The destination is selected by you and is governed by that destination's own privacy practices. We do not receive the exported file.

Notifications

The app asks for notification permission only when you choose to set reminders. You can change this permission in Android settings and turn reminders off in the app.

Advertising

The published version is planned to show advertising supplied by Google Mobile Ads. Google and its advertising partners may process device and advertising information to provide, measure, limit, or personalize ads where permitted. The consent choices shown in the app apply where required. This section will be reviewed against the final advertising configuration before publication.

Your choices

You can delete locally stored app data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. You can manage advertising privacy choices in the app when that option is available.

Children

Ten-Minute Home is not designed for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through an account or app server.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date and this policy will be updated before the change applies.

Contact

For support, use the developer contact email shown on the Ten-Minute Home Google Play listing.
